## Welcome!

Hi there! I'm **Juao Fan**, an undergraduate student majoring in Data Science at **BNU-HKBU United International College**.

This blog is where I'll share my learning journey, research notes, and thoughts on topics I find interesting in the world of AI and machine learning.

I believe writing helps deepen understanding. By explaining concepts in my own words, I can identify gaps in my knowledge and solidify what I've learned.
